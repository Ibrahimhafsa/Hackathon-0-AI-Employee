Conduct comprehensive market research to understand competitor strategies and identify market gaps. This research will inform our Q2 product roadmap decisions.

**From:** Product Team  
**Priority:** High  

## Details

The research should include:
- Competitive analysis of top 5 competitors

- Pricing strategy review

Document findings in a detailed report with recommendations for our product strategy.
